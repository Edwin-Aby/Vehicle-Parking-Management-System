# ParkWise — Smart Parking Management Web App

**Smart Parking. Smoother Journeys.**

A modern dark-themed web application with an interactive "Hey Driver" opening interface, 5 feature modules, and a GPT-6 Astra-style scroll-driven background where logo particles assemble as you scroll.

---

## 🚀 How to Open

### Option 1: Direct 1-Click Launch (Recommended)
- Simply double-click **`Open_In_Chrome.bat`**
- It will automatically launch **Google Chrome** with the app.

### Option 2: Open in Any Browser
- Double-click **`index.html`** or right-click `index.html` -> **Open with** -> **Google Chrome** (or Edge, Brave, Firefox).
- No internet server, Node.js, or installation required! Everything is 100% self-contained.

---

## 🎮 Interactive Features to Try

1. **"Hey Driver" Opening Interface**:
   - Location hub switcher (*Central Plaza*, *Metro Station*, *Cyber City*, *Airport*).
   - Quick reservation actions and 5 core feature cards from the design sheet (*Location Based*, *Check Availability*, *Safe & Secure*, *Real-time Availability*, *Hassle-free Parking*).

2. **GPT-6 Astra Scroll-Combining Background**:
   - Scroll up and down to watch 360 glowing emerald and mint particles gravitate and combine into the **ParkWise** logo.
   - Click the **"⚡ Auto-Combine Logo"** button in the topbar or sidebar to watch the animation loop automatically.
   - Drag the **Astra Combine** slider in the topbar to manually scrub between dispersed orbital particles and the fully combined logo.
   - Move your mouse across the screen to feel the interactive magnetic particle deflection.

3. **Interactive Parking Bays (Floor Plan)**:
   - Click **any slot** (A-01 to C-10):
     - **Green (Available)**: Opens bay details with 1-click booking.
     - **Red (Occupied)**: Opens vehicle ticket with 1-click exit settlement.

4. **1-Click Demo Buttons**:
   - In Vehicle Entry: Click **`⚡ Quick-Fill Demo Data`** to populate driver info instantly.
   - In Vehicle Exit: Click **`⚡ Pick Active Parked Car`** to test checkout, duration calculation, and payment (UPI/Card/Cash) with printable receipts.
